Permit Requirements for Commercial Shoots

Commercial photography and videography in the UAE is regulated. Event management companies commissioning professional shoots must comply with the following framework.

Shoot TypeGoverning AuthorityProcessing TimeIndicative Fee
General public location (commercial)Dubai Film & TV Commission (DFTC)3–7 working daysAED 1,500–2,500/location/day
Private location (hotel, mall, landmark)Property owner + DFTC5–10 daysAED 2,000–10,000+/day
Iconic landmarks (Burj Khalifa, Palm)DFTC + Emaar / NakheelUp to 10–25 daysAED 5,000–25,000/day
Aerial / drone photographyGCAA + DFTC5–15 daysAED 5,000+
Abu Dhabi shootsAbu Dhabi Film Commission (ADFC)5–15 days (incl. script review)Varies by project
Influencer / content creator (commercial)DFTC3–7 daysAED 500–2,500
Key Requirement: International production companies must appoint a licensed UAE production partner to apply for permits. Direct applications from overseas entities are not accepted. Documents required: script/storyboard, crew roster with passport details, equipment list, shooting schedule, and location list.
Important: Filming without a valid permit can result in equipment confiscation, fines, and potential legal proceedings. Sensitive zones — government buildings, military areas, and religious sites — require explicit special clearance.

Venue Selection Criteria & Planning Checklist

Use this checklist when evaluating venue options for clients. Thorough pre-booking due diligence reduces risk and aligns expectations.

Capacity & Layout
Confirm seated and standing capacity for event type (theatre / banquet / cocktail)
Verify room divisibility options for breakout sessions
Check column placement — pillar-free spaces preferred for large events
Confirm ceiling height for production rigging requirements
Technical & AV
In-house AV team vs. third-party provider (exclusivity clauses?)
Power supply specifications (kVA available for production)
Internet bandwidth for live-streamed events
Loading bay dimensions and goods access for set build
Catering & F&B
Exclusive in-house catering or approved external caterers
Cultural dietary requirements (halal, kosher, vegan)
Minimum F&B spend requirements
Corkage / outside beverage policy
Logistics
Parking capacity and valet services
Proximity to hotels for guest accommodation blocks
Airport transfer distances and metro/taxi access
Supplier access hours for setup and breakdown
Commercial
Venue hire fee vs. minimum spend model
Damage deposit and cancellation policy
VAT applicability (5% UAE VAT on most services)
Payment schedule and required advance deposit
Exclusivity — concurrent hire of adjacent spaces?
Brand & Compliance
Noise restrictions and curfew times
Outdoor event weather contingency / backup space
Photography and filming rights within the venue
Health & safety certifications and emergency procedures
