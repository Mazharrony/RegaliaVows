Important Information

Dubai Permit Guide

Understanding permit requirements in Dubai is essential for a seamless proposal. Here is everything you need to know — and everything we handle for you.

Public Locations
Photography, videography and special setups at public locations in Dubai — beaches, parks, public plazas — require a permit from Dubai Tourism (DTCM) or the relevant authority.

Processing time is typically 3–7 working days. We submit, follow up and confirm all approvals on your behalf.
Required for: JBR beach · Dubai Frame · public parks · DIFC outdoor areas · Jumeirah public beach
Private Venues
Hotels, private rooftops, restaurants and private beaches require direct venue manager approval rather than a government permit. This is typically faster — 24–48 hours — and sometimes involves a location fee.

We have pre-existing relationships with most of Dubai's top venues which significantly speeds this process.
Venue fees: AED 0–2,500 depending on venue and exclusivity. Always disclosed transparently in your quote.
Drone Permits
Drone flying in Dubai requires a permit from the General Civil Aviation Authority (GCAA). This is a separate application from standard photography permits.

Processing time: 7–14 days. Not all locations permit drone use. We advise on viability before confirming this add-on.
Not available near: Airports, military zones, certain residential areas, and within 5km of restricted airspace.
Desert & Remote Locations
Desert proposals outside Dubai municipality boundaries require coordination with the relevant emirate authority and landowner permission. We have established relationships in Al Marmoom and the Hajar Mountain foothills.

These locations offer the most privacy — and the most extraordinary experience.
Our commitment: We never book a location without confirmed written permission. Your proposal will never be interrupted.

What happens if the weather is bad on my proposal day?
+
Every Signature Vow and Regalia Experience booking includes a backup location on standby and a flexible rescheduling option. We monitor the weather from 72 hours before your date and communicate proactively. For outdoor proposals, we always have an indoor alternative prepared. You will never be left without a plan.
How do you keep the proposal a surprise without me being deceptive?
+
This is one of the most common concerns we hear — and we have helped hundreds of couples navigate it. We provide you with a complete "cover story" appropriate to the location (a "rooftop dinner reservation", a "sunset boat experience", a "birthday surprise") that feels natural and keeps the real plan hidden. We also coach you on how to answer her questions naturally in the days beforehand.
What is your deposit and cancellation policy?
+
We require a 50% deposit to confirm your booking, with the remaining 50% due 7 days before your proposal date. Cancellations more than 21 days before the event: full deposit refunded minus a AED 500 administration fee. Cancellations within 21 days: 50% of deposit is non-refundable. Cancellations within 7 days of the event: full booking value is non-refundable. Rescheduling is available once at no charge with more than 14 days notice.
Can I customise a package with specific elements not listed?
+
Absolutely. Every Regalia Vows proposal is built around your specific vision. If you have an idea — a particular flower she loves, a song that means everything to both of you, a message you want spelled out in candles, a specific restaurant you want to end the night at — tell us. We will build it into your experience. The packages are starting points, not ceilings.
Do you work with non-English speaking couples?
+
Yes. We work with couples from across the world and have served clients in Arabic, Hindi, French, Russian, and multiple other languages. We can arrange a coordinator who speaks your preferred language for the day of the proposal, and all our planning communication can be conducted in your language of choice.
What if she is not ready or I need to change the date?
+
Life happens — we understand completely. With more than 14 days notice, you can reschedule your booking once at no additional charge. If the new date requires a different venue or permit process, we will advise you of any additional costs before proceeding. Your deposit always moves with you to the new date.
Do I need to buy the ring before contacting you?
+
No — many of our clients come to us before the ring is ready. We can build your proposal timeline around your ring collection date. We can also connect you with our trusted jewellery partners in Dubai if you are still searching for the perfect ring. You do not need anything in hand to begin planning with us.
Can you help with the engagement party or wedding planning too?
+
We specialise in proposals and engagements. For wedding planning, we partner with some of Dubai's finest full-service wedding planners and will happily refer you to the right team for your next chapter. For engagement parties, anniversary events, and pre-wedding shoots — yes, we handle all of these as part of our extended services.
